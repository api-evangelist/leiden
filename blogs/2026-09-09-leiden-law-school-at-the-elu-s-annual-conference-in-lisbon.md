Twelve members from Leiden Law School contributed to the Annual Conference of the European Law Unbound Society (ELU-S) held from 1-3 September 2026 at the Universidade Católica Portuguesa in Lisbon. This year's theme was 'Words or Deeds? The European Moment', the conference explored how European values translate into practice amid profound transformation.
