Fatma Çapkurt and Sophie Starrenburg, who work at the Institute of Public Law, (Constitutional and Administrative Law and the Grotius Centre for International Legal Studies), have received a Veni grant of up to €320,000 for creative, ambitious and pioneering research. They can now develop their own line of research over the next three years.
