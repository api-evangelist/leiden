As the UNESCO World Heritage Committee meets to evaluate new sites, criticism of the consequences of UNESCO status is growing. Sophie Starrenburg, assistant professor of public international law and cultural heritage at the Grotius Centre, says UNESCO interests can conflict with local communities and put human rights under pressure.
