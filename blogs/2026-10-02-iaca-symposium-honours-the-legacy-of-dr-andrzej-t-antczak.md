From 12 to 18 July 2026, the XXXI International Congress of Caribbean Archaeology (IACA) took place in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, bringing together archaeologists and researchers from across the Caribbean and beyond. As part of the IACA, a special symposium took place honouring the legacy of the late Dr Andrzej Antczak.
