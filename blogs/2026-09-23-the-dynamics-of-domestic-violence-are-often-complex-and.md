As part of the SaFe Home research project, support organisations in Leiden are working together to treat domestic violence, with particular attention to psychological support. Healthcare psychologist and researcher Marie-Louise Kullberg was project leader. ‘Domestic violence in particular is something we should be treating in a more integrated way.’
