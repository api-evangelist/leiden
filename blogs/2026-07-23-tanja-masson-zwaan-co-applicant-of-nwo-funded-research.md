A collaborative research proposal on space infrastructure led by TU Delft has received funding of nearly €2 million from NWO, plus a little over €500,000 in co-funding from several partner organisations. Tanja Masson-Zwaan, an assistant professor in Air and Space Law, is involved in the project as a co-applicant on behalf of Leiden University.
