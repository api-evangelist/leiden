Journalists and FGW researchers gathered at the Academy Building on Monday 5 October. During a round-table discussion, they sought an answer to the question of how to maintain control over their coverage of Trump during the upcoming mid-term elections. ‘Realise that what might seem funny could cost people their lives.’
