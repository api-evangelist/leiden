Increasing resistance is making antibiotics less effective throughout the world, while they are indispensable for surgery and fighting infections. So, how can we carry on using antibiotics? Professor of Pharmacology Coen van Hasselt researches new therapies, such as smart ways of combining and personalising antibiotics.
