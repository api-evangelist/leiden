The House of Orange-Nassau actively supported Dutch colonialism for centuries, but only rarely determined colonial policy. This is the conclusion of an independent study presented to King Willem-Alexander on 2 October. ‘The spirit of the age is no excuse,’ commented principal researcher Gert Oostindie.
