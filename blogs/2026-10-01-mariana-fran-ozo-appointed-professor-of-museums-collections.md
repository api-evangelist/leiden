As of 1 October, Mariana Françozo has been appointed Professor of Museums, Collections and Society at Leiden University's Faculty of Archaeology. Her chair focuses on the relationships between museums, collections and society, bringing together perspectives from archaeology, heritage studies, anthropology and museum practice.
