Over a cup of tea, Gert Renkema and Niels Laurens discuss the financial position of the Faculty of Governance and Global Affairs. Their message is clear: FGGA is in good shape and continues to grow. At the same time, vacancies remain unfilled and limitations in BAS create extra work.
