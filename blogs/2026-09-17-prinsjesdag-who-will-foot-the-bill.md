The Miljoenennota (Budget Memorandum) shows households will bear most of the financial burden for 2027: purchasing power will decline slightly and higher incomes will pay an additional €750 million. Assistant professor Heike Vethaak: ‘Planning agencies warn of an accumulation of negative income effects, which could hit low-income households particularly hard.’
