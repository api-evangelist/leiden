Researchers at Leiden University and the University of Amsterdam have discovered that humans and songbirds recognise complex sounds differently. Zhi-Yuan Ning (formerly at IBL), Carel ten Cate (IBL) and their colleagues investigated this using a listening task in which humans and zebra finches were presented with the same artificial sounds.
