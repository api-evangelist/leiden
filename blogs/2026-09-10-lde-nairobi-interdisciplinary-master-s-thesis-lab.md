Are you passionate about addressing real-world challenges in Nairobi and Kenya? Do you want to collaborate with students from diverse disciplines and engage with local communities, policymakers, and practitioners? The LDE-East Africa Research Network (LEARN) Interdisciplinary Master's Thesis Lab is your chance to make an impact through your research while gaining hands-on experience in a dynamic, cross-cultural environment.
