At the General Assembly held during the 2026 ESIL Annual Conference in Málaga, Spain, Dr. Daniel Peat, Assistant Professor of International Law at Leiden University, was re-elected as of September 5, 2026, for a term of four year sto the Board of the European Society of International Law (ESIL).
