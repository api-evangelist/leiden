Women with an inherited BRCA1 mutation have an increased risk of breast and ovarian cancer. This type of tumour depends on the protein EXO1 to survive. Thanks to a KWF grant, Sebastian Pomplun, together with geneticist Sylvie Noordermeer, is investigating new inhibitors to disable this protein.
